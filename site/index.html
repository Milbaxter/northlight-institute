Northlight

Institute of Mental Health

We want to help people flourish.

Fear and anxiety can make life smaller. We’re interested in what helps people live with more freedom and wellbeing.

  • Care for the body through food, movement, and training.
  • Face fears instead of letting them set the limits.
  • Notice thoughts and loosen the ones that keep us stuck.
  • Stay curious. Try things. Change our minds.

Northlight is a long-term project. We’re figuring it out as we go, and we want to enjoy the work.
